About

Hi, I'm Rachel.

I'm the photographer behind RBG Photography, based in Woodstock, Maryland.

I started taking photos of my own family and friends, and word spread from there. What I love most is the in-between: the squeeze before a big laugh, a sibling whisper, the way a parent looks at their kids when nobody's watching.

My sessions are relaxed and playful. I'll give you just enough direction to feel comfortable, then let your family be your family. Kids are welcome to be kids. Wiggles, tears and silly faces all make it into the story.

When I'm not behind the camera you'll find me [a line about Rachel's life outside photography].

My approach

Natural light

I plan sessions around soft morning or golden-hour light, so skin looks warm and nobody's squinting.

Gentle guidance

Simple prompts and games instead of stiff poses. You'll never have to wonder what to do with your hands.

Kid-friendly pace

Breaks, snacks and silliness are part of the plan. Happy kids make the best photos.
