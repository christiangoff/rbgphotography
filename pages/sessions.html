Questions

Good to know

How do I reserve a date?

Send a request through the booking form. I'll reply with available dates, and a signed agreement plus a deposit holds your spot. The rest is due on the day of the session.

What should we wear?

Coordinating, not matching. Soft, earthy colors look lovely outdoors here. I'll send a style guide with ideas once you book.

What if it rains?

We'll watch the forecast together and reschedule for free if the weather doesn't cooperate.

When will we get our photos?

Galleries are usually ready within two to three weeks, delivered through a private online gallery where you can view, download and share.

Do you travel?

Sessions within about 25 miles of Woodstock have no travel fee. Farther out is welcome too; just ask.

Not sure which to pick?

Tell me a little about your family and what you're hoping for, and I'll suggest the best fit.
